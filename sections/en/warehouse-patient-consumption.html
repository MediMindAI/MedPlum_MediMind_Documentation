Ward Supply Management

The Ward Supply Management module (also known as Patient Consumption or Stationary) is the nurse's primary workstation for tracking every supply and procedure used on inpatient ward patients. Think of it as a detailed logbook where every bandage, syringe, medication, and medical procedure is recorded against a specific patient -- so the hospital knows exactly what was used, when, and for whom.

The interface is designed for speed and accuracy. Ward nurses typically operate under time pressure, so the layout puts the most frequently used controls -- patient selection, stock search, and expenditure entry -- within easy reach, without requiring page navigation or modal dialogs for routine tasks.

5 Screen Regions
2 Entry Modes
6 Quick-Action Buttons
5 Report Modals
FHIR Resources Used: Patient consumption data is stored using ChargeItem (each supply or procedure usage), Basic/StockQuant (department stock levels), Encounter (the patient's visit/admission), Location (ward assignments), and ActivityDefinition (procedure templates with pre-defined supply lists).

1.1 Page Layout and Navigation

The page is divided into five distinct regions, each serving a specific role in the consumption workflow. The layout mirrors a traditional hospital ward workstation -- filters on the left, patients in the center, stock on the right, and the entry/history area at the bottom.

Title Bar

Hospital icon + page title + selected patient's registration number and name

Left: Filters

Department, Date, Time sliders

Center: Patient List

Patients + Transfer history

Right: Stock Panel

Actions, Search, Inventory

Bottom Left: Expenditure Entry

Mode selector, search, history list

Bottom Right: Event Details

Procedure sub-items, pricing, totals

Status Bar

Pulsing green dot + "Online (N)" live indicator

Title Bar

The title bar spans the full width of the page with a gradient background. It shows a hospital icon and the page title. When a patient is selected from the center list, their registration number and full name appear in the title bar, giving the nurse a constant reminder of who they are currently working with.

Status Bar

At the very bottom of the page, a thin status bar shows a pulsing green dot and the text "Online (N)" where N is the number of patients currently loaded in the patient list. This confirms the connection is live and data is up to date.

Typical Workflow

  1. Select a department from the left sidebar filter (defaults to Emergency)
  2. Click a patient in the center list to select them
  3. In the bottom-left, search for a supply item or procedure
  4. Set the quantity and click the Add button (or press Enter)
  5. The item appears in the expenditure history; stock decreases automatically

1.2 Filters Sidebar (Left Column)

The left sidebar (260px fixed width) provides three filter controls that determine which patients and stock you see. It has a distinctive accent-blue left border for quick visual identification.

Control What It Does Details
Department Dropdown Filters which ward's patients appear in the center list Searchable, clearable. Auto-selects the "Emergency" department on first load (matches by keyword in Georgian, English, or Russian)
Date Picker Sets the "as-of" date for viewing patient admissions Inline calendar widget. Defaults to today's date. Changing the date reloads the patient list to show who was admitted on or before that date
Time Display + Sliders Fine-tunes the exact time for the "as-of" timestamp Shows the current selected time as HH:MM. The Hour slider ranges from 0 to 23; the Minute slider ranges from 0 to 59. Defaults to 23:59 (end of day)
Time Slider Debounce: Dragging a time slider does not fire an API call on every pixel of movement. The system waits 500 milliseconds after you stop dragging before fetching the updated patient list -- this prevents unnecessary server load while still feeling responsive.

Department Syncing

When you change the department filter, two things happen simultaneously:

  • The patient list updates to show patients admitted to the selected ward
  • The stock panel (right column) automatically switches to show the same department's inventory

This sync works because wards (FHIR Location resources) and stock departments (FHIR Organization resources) are mapped by name. Selecting "Emergency" in the patient filter automatically selects "Emergency" in the stock department dropdown.

1.3 Patient List (Center Column)

The center column shows all patients currently admitted to the selected department. It consists of two parts: the main patient table and, when a patient is selected, a transfer history mini-table below it.

Patient Table

The table has three columns:

Column Content Details
# (Badge) Sequential row number Displayed as a circular numbered badge. The selected patient's badge changes to a highlighted style
Patient Name Full name of the patient Takes up 55% of the table width
Personal ID 11-digit Georgian Personal ID Takes up 30% of the table width

Click a patient row to select them. The row highlights with a selection color, and the patient's name appears in the title bar above. Click the same row again to deselect. Only one patient can be selected at a time.

The panel header shows a patient count badge (e.g., "12") reflecting how many patients are in the current filtered list. If no patients match the filter, an empty state with an icon and message appears instead of the table.

Transfer History

When a patient is selected, a secondary table appears below the patient list showing their department transfer history -- every ward they have been assigned to during their current admission. This is useful for tracking a patient who has moved between departments (e.g., from Emergency to Surgery to Recovery).

Column Content
# Transfer sequence number
Department Name of the ward or department
Date Date of the transfer
FHIR Mapping: Patient list data comes from Encounter resources with status "in-progress" or "finished", filtered by the ward's Location ID. Transfer history is derived from the Encounter's location history entries.

1.4 Stock Panel (Right Column)

The right column is the department's inventory view. It shows what supplies are currently available in the selected department and provides quick-access buttons for reports and calculations. It has a primary navy left border.

Department Dropdown

At the top, a dropdown lets you choose which department's stock to view. This is separate from the patient filter on the left -- you can view one department's patients while checking another department's stock, though typically they stay synced.

Action Buttons Row

Below the department dropdown, a row of action buttons provides quick access to modals and reports:

Button Label What It Opens Requires Patient?
Calculator icon Calculation Full cost calculation modal (3 tabs) for the selected patient Yes
"H" button Department History Modal showing all patients grouped by department with admission dates No
"O" button Nurse Supply Modal showing supply delivery history for the current ward No
Daily Balance Daily Balance Modal with daily consumption balance table and print buttons No
Measure Treatment/Research Modal with two tabs (Supplies & Medications, Research & Consultations) showing the patient's expenditure summary Yes
Refresh icon Refresh Stock Reloads the stock list from the server No

Stock Search and Item List

Below the action buttons, a search bar with a refresh button lets you filter stock items by name or item code. The search filters the list in real-time as you type.

The stock table shows three columns:

  • Item Name -- the display name, which includes the barcode, product name, supplier, batch number, and expiry date (formatted as: *barcode* name *supplier* batch (expiry))
  • Quantity -- shown as a badge. When quantity drops to 5 or below, the badge turns red to alert the nurse that the item is running low
  • Unit -- the unit of measure (e.g., "each", "pack", "mL")

Click a stock item to select it for adding to a patient's expenditure. The item row highlights, and its name auto-fills into the search field in the bottom-left expenditure entry area.

Low Stock Warning: When a stock item's quantity badge turns red (5 or fewer remaining), consider notifying the warehouse to arrange a transfer or restock. Running out of a supply mid-shift can impact patient care.

1.5 Expenditure Entry and History (Bottom Zone)

The bottom zone is split into two columns and is where the actual supply tracking happens. The left side is for adding and viewing expenditures, while the right side shows details for a selected procedure.

Bottom Left: Expenditure Entry

The header bar shows three elements: a mode selector dropdown, an icon, and the current date. Below it, a search row with three controls handles item entry:

Control Purpose Behavior
Search Input Find items or procedures to add Shows autocomplete dropdown with matching results. In Expenditure mode, shows stock items with quantity and unit. In Procedure mode, shows procedures with service code, name, and price in ₾
Quantity Input Set how many units to record Number input with minimum of 1. Defaults to 1 after each entry
Add Button (+) Submit the expenditure Disabled until both a patient and an item/procedure are selected. Shows a loading spinner during submission. You can also press Enter in the search or quantity field to submit

Two Entry Modes

The mode selector dropdown switches between two types of expenditure entry:

Mode What It Records Stock Impact Autocomplete Shows
Expenditure Physical supplies used on the patient (bandages, syringes, medications) Decreases the department's stock by the entered quantity Stock items with: display name, available quantity, and unit (e.g., "Gauze Pad 10x10 (50 each)")
Procedure Medical procedures performed on the patient (lab tests, imaging, consultations) No stock decrement -- procedures are service charges, not physical items Procedure catalog items with: service code, name, and price in ₾ (e.g., "24001 - Blood Test (15.00 ₾)")

Switching between modes clears the search field and any selected item, so you always start fresh. The history list below also filters to show only the relevant type -- expenditures show only supplies, procedures show only procedures.

Expenditure History List

Below the search row, a scrollable list shows all recorded expenditures for the selected patient (filtered by the current mode). Each row shows:

  • Item Name -- the supply or procedure name. Procedure rows appear in bold blue text and are clickable
  • Quantity + Unit -- how much was used (e.g., "2 each")
  • Delete button -- a trash icon that, when clicked, shows a confirmation dialog before removing the entry and restoring the stock
Optimistic Updates: When you add or delete an expenditure, the change appears instantly in the list without waiting for the server response. If the server request fails, the system automatically reverts the change and shows an error notification.

Bottom Right: Event Details

When you click a procedure row in the expenditure history (bold blue text), the right panel loads that procedure's sub-items -- the specific supplies that were consumed as part of that procedure.

The header shows the procedure name. Below it, a search row appears (identical to the left side) that lets you add additional supply items to the selected procedure. The detail table shows:

Column Content
Item Name of the supply used in the procedure
Qty Quantity consumed
Unit Price Cost per unit in GEL
Total Price Quantity multiplied by unit price
Delete Remove this sub-item (with confirmation)

At the bottom of the event details panel, a total sum row with a gradient background shows the combined cost of all sub-items in GEL.

Stock Validation

The system performs client-side stock validation before submitting an expenditure. If you try to record more units than are currently available, a warning notification appears showing the requested quantity and the available quantity. The expenditure is not submitted until the quantity is reduced to an available level.

FHIR Mapping: Each expenditure creates a ChargeItem resource linked to the patient's Encounter. Procedure sub-items are also ChargeItem resources, connected to their parent procedure via a parentChargeItemId extension. Stock decrements update the corresponding Basic (StockQuant) resource.

1.6 Procedure Templates

Some procedures come with a pre-defined list of supplies that are typically consumed during that procedure -- called an expenditure template. For example, a "Wound Suturing" procedure might have a template listing: suture thread, sterile gloves, gauze pads, and antiseptic solution.

When you add a procedure that has a template, the system automatically:

  1. Looks up the procedure's template (stored in the ActivityDefinition FHIR resource)
  2. Matches each template item against the department's current stock by name
  3. Opens a Template Confirmation Modal showing the results

Template Confirmation Modal

The modal presents a table where each row represents one item from the procedure's template:

Column Content Details
Checkbox Select/deselect item Checked by default for available items. Disabled (unchecked) for items not found in stock
Item Name Template item name + matched stock item Shows the generic template name (e.g., "Gauze Pad 10x10") and below it the matched stock item's full display name (with barcode, supplier, batch)
Template Qty Default quantity from the template Read-only -- the suggested amount
Available Current stock quantity Shows the actual amount in stock, or a dash if the item was not found
Final Qty Editable quantity to actually consume Number input. The nurse can adjust up or down. Cannot exceed the available quantity
Status Availability badge Green "Available", orange "Insufficient" (stock is less than template qty), or gray "Not Found" (item not in department stock)

Items with a "Not Found" status appear at 50% opacity with a disabled checkbox -- they cannot be added because the department does not have that supply in stock. The nurse should arrange a transfer from the warehouse for those items.

Click Confirm to add all checked items as sub-items of the procedure. Each confirmed item decrements the department stock and creates a linked ChargeItem resource.

Template Is a Suggestion: The template provides a starting point, not a fixed requirement. Nurses can uncheck items they did not use, adjust quantities, or skip the modal entirely by closing it. The procedure itself is already recorded regardless of what happens with the template.

1.7 Calculations and Reports

The Ward Supply module includes several report modals accessible from the action buttons in the stock panel. These modals provide financial summaries, department overviews, and printable reports.

Calculation Modal (Calculator Button)

The calculation modal provides a complete financial breakdown of all supplies and procedures consumed by the selected patient during their admission. It has three tabs:

Tab Content Use Case
Internal Calculation Two sections: "Research & Consultations" (procedures) and "Supplies & Medications" (expenditures). Each section groups items by department with subtotals Detailed cost breakdown for billing or internal audit
By Departments All items (both research and supplies) merged and grouped by department name. Each department shows its items with a department subtotal Department-level cost comparison -- which ward used the most resources
Intraoperative Items specifically tagged as intraoperative consumption (used during surgical procedures) Surgical cost accounting, theater supply tracking

Each tab displays a table with six columns: # (row number), Name, Unit, Quantity, Unit Price, and Total. Section headers appear in a distinct background color, and subtotals appear at the end of each section. A grand total box at the bottom shows the combined cost in GEL.

A Print button opens the calculation in a new browser window formatted for printing, including the patient name, admission date, and discharge date (if applicable).

Department History Modal ("H" Button)

Shows all current inpatients grouped by their department. Each group lists patients with their admission date, Personal ID, first name, and last name. This is useful for getting a hospital-wide overview of where patients are distributed.

Nurse Supply Modal ("O" Button)

Shows the supply delivery history for the currently filtered department. Each row displays: Date, Description (what was delivered), Supplier, and Quantity. This helps nurses see when and what supplies were last delivered to their ward.

Treatment/Research Modal (Measure Button)

A summary modal for the selected patient's expenditure data, split into two tabs:

  • Supplies & Medications tab -- lists all physical supply expenditures (non-procedure items) with date, name, and quantity
  • Research & Consultations tab -- lists all procedure entries with date, name, and quantity

This provides a quick patient-level view without the full financial detail of the Calculation modal.

Daily Balance Modal (Daily Balance Button)

Shows the day's consumption balance for the selected stock department. The table displays: Name, Quantity, and Amount (cost in GEL). At the bottom, three print buttons offer different report scopes:

Print Button What It Prints
Print Today Only today's consumption data
Print Total Cumulative consumption for the entire admission period
Print Combined Both today's and total data combined in one printout

1.8 Data Model Reference

For the technical integration team, the following table maps the Ward Supply module's key data fields to their underlying FHIR resources and paths.

Data FHIR Resource Path / Extension
Patient admission Encounter Encounter.status (in-progress/finished), Encounter.location
Ward assignment Location Location.physicalType = "wa" (ward), Location.managingOrganization
Stock department Organization Organization.id, Organization.name
Stock quantity Basic (StockQuant) Tag: department CodeSystem with Organization ID. Extensions prefixed sq-
Supply expenditure ChargeItem ChargeItem.code (item code), ChargeItem.quantity, ChargeItem.occurrenceDateTime
Procedure entry ChargeItem Same as supply, but with a procedure-type code in ChargeItem.code.coding
Procedure sub-item ChargeItem Extension: parentChargeItemId linking to the parent procedure's ChargeItem ID
Procedure template ActivityDefinition Template items stored in the ActivityDefinition's extensions
Stock lot tracking ChargeItem extension Extension: ward-stock-lot-number
FHIR Base URL: All FHIR extension URLs use the base http://medimind.ge/fhir (HTTP, not HTTPS). Currency values use GEL (Georgian Lari).
