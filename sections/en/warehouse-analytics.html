Balance Analytics

The Balance Analytics page is the most comprehensive reporting tool in the MediMind warehouse module. Think of it as your hospital's supply chain intelligence center -- it takes the raw transaction data (receipts, transfers, write-offs, dispensing) and transforms it into actionable insights across 9 specialized tabs.

Hospital managers use this page to answer critical questions: "Where is our money going? Which items are sitting idle? What will we run out of next month? Which suppliers deliver on time?" Instead of pulling reports from spreadsheets, everything is available in real-time from a single page.

9 Analytics Tabs
8 KPI Cards
8 Date Presets
22+ Table Columns
Export Options: Every analytics view can be exported. Use the Excel button for raw data analysis in spreadsheets, or the PDF button for formatted reports suitable for management meetings and audits.

1.1 Global Filters

At the top of the analytics page, a collapsible filter bar controls which data appears in all tabs below. Setting filters here applies them across every tab -- you do not need to re-filter when switching between Balance Table, Insights, or any other view.

Date Range

Two date pickers let you define the analysis period. The "From" date sets the starting point and the "To" date sets the end. The system automatically prevents you from selecting an end date before the start date.

Quick Presets

Below the date pickers, a row of pill-shaped buttons provides one-click shortcuts for common time ranges. Clicking a preset automatically fills both date fields:

Preset Date Range Applied Typical Use
Today Current date only End-of-day stock check
This Week Monday through today Weekly activity review
This Month 1st of current month through today Monthly reporting
Last Month Full previous calendar month Completed month comparison
This Quarter Start of current quarter through today Quarterly budget review
Last 3 Months Rolling 90-day window Trend analysis
Last 6 Months Rolling 180-day window Seasonal patterns
This Year January 1st through today Annual performance review

Department and Group Filters

Two multi-select dropdowns let you narrow the analysis to specific departments or categories:

  • Department -- Select one or more hospital departments (e.g., Emergency, Surgery, ICU). The dropdown is searchable and supports clearing all selections
  • Category -- Select one or more item categories (e.g., Medications, Consumables, Reagents). Useful for analyzing a specific category of supplies

An active filter count badge appears on the filter bar showing how many filters are currently applied, so you always know when your data is filtered.

Click "Search" to Apply: After changing any filters, you must click the Search button to apply them. Simply selecting a date or department does not automatically reload the data -- this is intentional to prevent unnecessary server load when setting multiple filters.

1.2 KPI Summary Strip

Once you run a search, a row of KPI cards appears below the filters, giving you an instant health check of your warehouse for the selected period. The cards are arranged in two rows of four:

KPI Card What It Shows How to Read It
Total Inventory Value The total monetary value of all items currently in stock (in GEL) A rising value without corresponding activity may indicate overstocking
Total Items Count of distinct items that had any movement in the period Compare against your full catalog to see how many items are active
Total Purchases Total monetary value of all purchases (receipts from suppliers) Track against budget to monitor procurement spending
Total Dispensed Total value of items dispensed to patients or consumed The core measure of how much supplies support patient care
Dead Stock Items Number of items with zero movement but positive balance High numbers indicate capital tied up in unused inventory
Low Stock Items Number of items running below safe supply levels These items need reordering attention before they run out
Avg Days of Supply Average number of days current stock will last at current consumption rates Below 14 days suggests system-wide supply risk
Turnover Rate How many times inventory is "turned over" (sold and replaced) in the period Higher is generally better -- it means supplies flow efficiently

When available, a ninth card shows the Fill Rate -- the percentage of requested items that were successfully fulfilled. A fill rate below the benchmark threshold appears in red, signaling fulfillment problems.

FHIR Data Sources: KPI values are computed from Basic (StockQuant) resources for current balances, SupplyDelivery for movement history (receipts, transfers, write-offs), and SupplyRequest for order fulfillment rates. Data is cached for 30 seconds to balance accuracy with performance.

1.3 Balance Table

The Balance Table tab is the most data-dense view in the analytics system. It presents a comprehensive ledger showing every item's opening balance, all inflows and outflows, and closing balance for the selected period -- similar to a financial trial balance, but for inventory.

Color-Coded Column Groups

The table organizes its 22+ columns into color-coded groups so you can quickly scan for the information you need:

Column Group Color Columns What It Tracks
Item Info No color Code, Name, Unit Identifies the item. Code and Name columns are "sticky" (they stay visible when scrolling horizontally)
Opening Balance Blue tint Opening Qty, Avg Price, Opening Amount The stock level at the start of your selected date range
Inflows Green tint Purchase Qty/Amount, Transfer In Qty/Amount Stock coming in: purchases from suppliers and transfers from other departments
Outflows Orange tint Return Qty/Amount, Transfer Out Qty/Amount, Write-Off Qty/Amount, Dispensed Qty/Amount, Write-Off + Dispensed Stock going out: returns to suppliers, transfers to other departments, write-offs, and items dispensed to patients
Closing Balance Purple tint Closing Qty, Closing Amount The stock level at the end of your selected date range
Analytics No color Days of Supply, ABC Class Calculated metrics: how long stock will last and its importance classification

Row Highlighting

The table automatically highlights rows that need attention:

  • Red-tinted rows -- Items with a closing quantity of zero (stock-out). These items have run out and may be affecting patient care
  • Yellow-tinted rows -- Dead stock items: they have positive closing balance but zero movement in the period. Capital is tied up in these items without any usage

Days of Supply Badges

The Days of Supply column uses a traffic-light system to show urgency:

  • Red badge (<7 days) -- Critical. At current consumption rates, stock will run out within a week
  • Yellow badge (7-30 days) -- Caution. Stock is limited but not immediately critical
  • Green badge (>30 days) -- Healthy. Sufficient stock for at least a month
  • Gray "N/A" badge -- Item has stock but zero dispensing activity, so days of supply cannot be calculated
  • "999+" display -- More than 999 days of supply (effectively overstocked)

ABC Classification Badges

Each item automatically receives an ABC class based on its dispensing value relative to all items:

  • A (red badge) -- Top 80% of total dispensing value. These are your most valuable items and deserve the closest management attention
  • B (yellow badge) -- Next 15% of value. Important but less critical than A items
  • C (green badge) -- Remaining 5% of value. Low-value items that can be managed with simpler controls

Inline Search and Totals

  • Code search and Name search inputs above the table let you filter items without changing the global filters
  • A sticky totals row at the bottom sums all quantity and amount columns, scrolling horizontally in sync with the main table
  • Column headers have tooltips explaining what each column measures -- hover over any header to see the description

1.4 Insights

The Insights tab presents four summary views that help managers quickly understand warehouse activity patterns without diving into the raw data. Think of it as the "executive summary" of your inventory operations.

Top 10 Items by Value

A ranked list showing the ten most valuable items in your inventory. Each item displays its name, total value, and a proportional progress bar (the #1 item fills the full bar width, and all others scale relative to it). This immediately answers: "Where is most of our inventory capital concentrated?"

Movement Summary

A six-row table aggregating all stock movements across the selected period:

Movement Type What It Represents
Purchases Total quantity and value received from suppliers
Transfers In Items received from other departments
Transfers Out Items sent to other departments
Returns Items returned to suppliers
Write-Offs Items removed due to expiry, damage, or other losses
Dispensed Items consumed for patient care

Period Comparison

Compares the current period against the previous period of equal length. For example, if you selected "This Month," it compares against last month. Three key metrics are compared:

  • Purchases -- Are we buying more or less than before?
  • Dispensed -- Is consumption going up or down?
  • Total Inventory Value -- Is our stock value growing or shrinking?

Each metric shows a percentage change badge: green for increases, red for decreases, and blue for no change.

Department Activity

A visual breakdown of inflow vs. outflow per department. Each department shows two horizontal bars:

  • Green bar -- Inflow (value of supplies received by the department)
  • Yellow bar -- Outflow (value of supplies sent out or consumed)

Departments where the outflow bar significantly exceeds the inflow bar may be consuming faster than they are being resupplied.

1.5 Dead Stock & Low Stock

These two tabs identify items at opposite ends of the inventory health spectrum: items that are not moving at all (dead stock) and items that are dangerously low (low stock). Both tables are sortable by any column.

Dead Stock Tab

Dead stock items have positive closing balance (they are in your warehouse) but had absolutely no movement during the selected period -- no purchases, no transfers, no dispensing, nothing. They represent capital that is sitting idle.

Column Description
Code / Name / Unit Item identification
Closing Qty How many units are sitting in stock
Closing Amount The monetary value of idle stock (in GEL)
Days Since Last Movement How many days since any transaction occurred. Items that have never had any movement display a red "Never Moved" badge
Action on Dead Stock: Items showing "Never Moved" may have been incorrectly entered or received into the wrong department. Review these items for potential data errors, and consider transferring idle items to departments that may need them before they expire.

Low Stock Tab

Low stock items have positive balance but their closing quantity is below 5 units. While the Days of Supply column shows how many days the current stock will last, the low stock filter itself identifies items with closing quantity below this threshold.

The Days of Supply column uses color-coded text:

  • Red text (<7 days) -- Critical shortage. Reorder immediately
  • Yellow text (7-30 days) -- Low but not critical. Plan reorder soon
  • Green text (>30 days) -- Healthy supply level
  • "999+" -- Effectively unlimited supply at current consumption (possible overstocking)

1.6 ABC/XYZ Classification

The ABC/XYZ tab applies a well-known inventory management technique that classifies every item along two dimensions: how much it costs (ABC) and how predictably it is consumed (XYZ). The result is a 3x3 matrix that tells you which items need the most management attention.

Understanding the Axes

Axis Class Definition What It Means
ABC (Value) A Top 80% of annual spend High-value items. A relatively small number of items that account for most of your spending
B Next 15% of annual spend Medium-value items. Important but not as critical as A items
C Remaining 5% of annual spend Low-value items. Many individual items but little total spend
XYZ (Variability) X Coefficient of Variation < 0.1 Stable demand. Consumption is very predictable week to week
Y CV between 0.1 and 0.25 Moderate variability. Some fluctuation but generally predictable
Z CV > 0.25 Volatile demand. Consumption varies significantly -- hard to forecast

The Interactive Matrix

The 3x3 grid is clickable. Each cell shows the number of items in that classification and their combined annual spend. Click any cell to filter the table below to show only items in that classification. Click again to deselect.

Cells are color-coded by management priority -- cells in the upper-left (AX: high value, stable demand) are highlighted differently from cells in the lower-right (CZ: low value, volatile demand).

Drill-Down Table

Below the matrix, a sortable table lists individual items with their details:

Column Description
Item Code The item's catalog code
Item Name Product name
Annual Spend Total spend on this item in the period (in GEL)
CV Coefficient of Variation -- a statistical measure of demand variability. Lower is more predictable
Class Combined classification badge (e.g., AX, BY, CZ) with color coding
Management Strategy A recommended approach for managing this type of item (e.g., "Just-in-time ordering" for AX items)
Minimum Data Required: ABC/XYZ classification requires at least 3 months of transaction history to produce meaningful results. If insufficient data is available, the tab displays an empty state explaining the requirement.

1.7 Expiration Risk

The Expiration Risk tab helps prevent waste by showing which items are approaching their expiry date. Items are grouped into five time buckets so you can prioritize action based on urgency.

Time Bucket Cards

Five clickable cards across the top show how many items fall into each urgency window:

Bucket Color Urgency Recommended Action
7 Days Red Critical Use immediately, transfer to departments that need them, or prepare for write-off
14 Days Orange Urgent Prioritize for use in upcoming procedures. Alert departments
30 Days Yellow Caution Plan usage or redistribution within the month
60 Days Blue Monitor Be aware but no immediate action needed
90 Days Green Watch Low priority -- include in routine expiry management

Each card shows both the item count and the total value at risk (the monetary value of items that may be lost if they expire unused).

Drill-Down Table

Click any bucket card to reveal a detailed table of items in that time window:

  • Item Code / Name -- Item identification
  • Lot Number -- The specific lot that is expiring
  • Department -- Where the item is currently stored
  • Quantity / Unit Cost / Value at Risk -- The financial impact if the item expires
  • Expiry Date -- The actual expiration date
  • Days Left badge -- Color-coded: red for 7 days or less, yellow for 8-30 days, blue for more than 30 days

Department Summary

Below the table, a horizontal bar chart shows how expiration risk is distributed across departments. This helps identify if one department is holding a disproportionate share of soon-to-expire items.

1.8 Suppliers & Operations

The Suppliers & Operations tab provides four distinct analysis sections stacked vertically. Together they answer: "Who are our suppliers? How do they perform? Where are we losing stock? Which departments cost the most?"

Supplier Scorecard

A ranked table of all suppliers, sorted by total spend (highest first). Click any column header to re-sort. Each supplier row shows:

Column Description
Rank (#) Position based on current sort order
Supplier Name The supplier's organization name
Delivery Count Number of deliveries received in the period
Total Spend Total monetary value of purchases from this supplier (in GEL)
Item Variety Number of distinct items supplied -- a high variety means this supplier covers many product categories
Avg Lead Time Average number of days between order and delivery. Shorter is better
Price Trend Badge showing whether this supplier's prices are increasing (red), stable (yellow), or decreasing (green)

Click any supplier row to expand and see their recent deliveries -- a sub-table showing the date, item count, and total value of each recent delivery.

Stockout Log

A chronological record of every time an item reached zero stock. This section includes:

  • Item Name / Code -- Which item ran out
  • Department -- Where the stockout occurred. Use the department filter dropdown to focus on a specific department
  • Date Reached Zero -- When the item went to zero stock
  • Date Restocked -- When stock was restored (or a dash if still empty)
  • Duration -- How long the stockout lasted. For ongoing stockouts, a pulsing red "Ongoing" badge appears instead of a duration

Use the sort toggle button to switch between sorting by date (most recent first) and by duration (longest outage first).

Department Cost Ranking

Departments ranked by their total supply consumption cost, with proportional bar charts (the top-spending department fills the full bar width). Each department row shows total cost, item count, and fill rate. Click to expand and see the department's top items with cost breakdown and percentage of the department's total spend.

Write-Off Analysis

A visual breakdown of losses by reason, featuring three components:

  • Donut chart -- A circular chart showing the proportion of write-offs by reason (Expired, Damaged, Theft, Correction, Other). The center shows the total value lost
  • Legend -- Clickable rows listing each reason with its quantity, value, and percentage of total write-offs. Click a reason to drill down
  • Monthly trend bars -- A grouped bar chart showing write-off values per month, color-coded by reason. This reveals whether losses are increasing or decreasing over time
  • Drill-down table -- When a reason is selected from the legend, a table appears listing every individual write-off item with its code, name, quantity, value, date, and department

1.9 Clinical

The Clinical tab bridges the gap between warehouse operations and patient care. It links supply consumption to clinical data -- diagnoses, hospital encounters, and lot numbers -- enabling cost analysis and recall traceability that would be impossible from inventory records alone.

This tab contains three sub-tabs:

Diagnosis Cost Analysis

Shows supply spending grouped by ICD-10 diagnosis code. For each diagnosis, you see the total supply cost, number of encounters, and average cost per encounter. This answers: "Which diagnoses consume the most supplies?"

Key features:

  • Outlier detection -- Diagnoses whose total cost exceeds the mean + 2 standard deviations are flagged with a red "Outlier" badge. This uses statistical detection (mean + 2 sigma) to identify diagnoses with unusually high supply costs
  • Expandable rows -- Click the arrow on any diagnosis to reveal its top items: the specific supplies that drive the cost, with quantity and cost per item
  • Outlier-highlighted rows -- Rows flagged as outliers receive a subtle visual highlight to draw attention
Column Description
Diagnosis Code ICD-10 code with display name below
Encounter Count How many patient encounters used this diagnosis
Total Cost Combined supply cost across all encounters with this diagnosis
Avg Cost / Encounter Average supply cost per encounter -- useful for budgeting per case type

Encounter Cost Report

Shows supply spending per patient encounter (hospital stay), sorted by total cost. This reveals which individual patient stays consumed the most supplies.

  • Patient Name and Department -- Identifies the encounter
  • Duration (Days) -- Length of stay
  • Total Cost -- All supplies consumed during this encounter
  • Outlier badge -- Statistical outlier detection flags encounters with unusually high supply costs
  • Expandable detail -- Click to see every supply item consumed: name, quantity, cost, and date

Lot Recall

A traceability tool for tracking a specific lot number across the entire hospital. This is critical for safety recalls: when a manufacturer recalls a lot, you need to know exactly where it went and which patients received it.

How to use it:

  1. Enter a lot number in the search field
  2. Click Search (or press Enter)
  3. The system returns a result card showing:
    • Item details -- The product name and code associated with this lot
    • Total affected patients count (displayed as a red badge)
    • Department breakdown table -- For each department that received this lot: quantity received, quantity remaining, quantity dispensed to patients, and whether remaining stock exists
    • Quarantine recommendation -- Departments still holding this lot show a yellow "Quarantine Recommended" badge
    • Affected patients table -- Lists every patient who received items from this lot: patient name, department, quantity, and date
Critical Safety Feature: The Lot Recall tool is essential for regulatory compliance. When a manufacturer issues a recall, this tool provides the complete chain of custody: from receipt at the warehouse, through department distribution, to individual patient administration. Departments with remaining stock should immediately quarantine the affected items.

1.10 Planning

The Planning tab provides forward-looking tools for supply chain management: budget tracking, automated reorder triggers, and demand forecasting. It contains three sub-tabs.

Budget vs. Actual

Compare planned budgets against actual spending per department. This is the financial control center for supply costs.

The variance table shows each department's budget, actual spend, variance amount, and variance percentage. Color coding immediately highlights the situation:

  • Red text + "Over Budget" badge -- Department has spent more than its allocated budget. Variance shows the overage
  • Green text + "Under Budget" badge -- Department is within budget. Variance shows the remaining amount

To set or update a department's budget, click the "Set Budget" button to open a modal dialog where you select:

  • Department -- Choose from a searchable dropdown
  • Month -- Select the target month using a month picker (defaults to the current month)
  • Budget Amount -- Enter the amount in GEL

Reorder Points

A statistically calculated list of items with their optimal reorder triggers. The system analyzes historical consumption patterns to recommend when and how much to reorder.

When items are below their reorder threshold, an alert banner appears at the top showing the count of items needing reorder.

Column Description
Item Name / Code Item identification
Safety Stock The minimum quantity to keep on hand as a buffer against demand spikes
Reorder Point When stock falls to this level, place a new order. Calculated from lead time and average consumption
Suggested Order Qty The recommended quantity to order based on economic order quantity calculations
Current Stock How much is currently on hand
Needs Reorder Red "Needs Reorder" badge if current stock is at or below the reorder point, otherwise green "OK"
Confidence How reliable the calculation is: High (green, 90+ days of data), Medium (yellow, 30-90 days), Low (red, less than 30 days of history)

Demand Forecast

A predictive view showing expected consumption for each item over the next 30, 60, and 90 days. Think of it as the system answering: "Based on how fast we have been using this item, how much will we need next month?"

Column Description
Item Name / Code Item identification, plus the number of months of historical data available
History (Sparkline) A miniature bar chart showing monthly consumption history. Each bar represents one month, and the bar height is proportional to the quantity consumed. This gives a visual sense of the consumption trend
30d / 60d / 90d Predicted quantity needed for each forecast horizon
Confidence Range The statistical range (low estimate to high estimate) for the 90-day forecast. A narrow range means high confidence
Trend Badge with direction arrow: "Increasing" (yellow, consumption is going up), "Stable" (green, consistent), or "Decreasing" (blue, consumption is going down)
Forecast Accuracy: Forecasts are more reliable for items with longer consumption history and stable demand (X-class items in ABC/XYZ). For volatile items (Z-class), pay more attention to the confidence range than the point estimate. Items with less than 6 months of history may not appear in the forecast.

1.11 FHIR Resource Mapping

For the integration team, the analytics system draws data from several FHIR resources. Understanding these mappings is useful when building custom reports, troubleshooting data discrepancies, or integrating with external analytics tools.

Analytics Feature FHIR Resource Key Fields Used
Current Balances / KPIs Basic (StockQuant) Extensions: sq-item-code, sq-location-id, sq-quantity, sq-unit-cost, sq-expiry-date, sq-lot-number
Movement History SupplyDelivery type (receipt/transfer/writeoff), quantity, suppliedItem, occurrenceDateTime, supplier
Orders / Fill Rate SupplyRequest status, quantity, authoredOn, requester, deliverFrom, deliverTo
Item Master / ABC/XYZ DeviceDefinition identifier, deviceName, classification (group code), extensions for attributes
Clinical Costs / Diagnosis Encounter + Condition Encounter.period, Encounter.location, Condition.code (ICD-10), linked SupplyDelivery resources
Lot Recall / Traceability SupplyDelivery + Basic (StockQuant) SupplyDelivery lot extension, destination (Patient reference), StockQuant sq-lot-number
Budgets Basic (Budget) Custom resource with department reference, period (month), budget-amount extension
Departments Location / Organization Location for ward assignments, Organization for stock tracking and department hierarchy
Performance Note: Analytics queries use indexed searches on FHIR extensions and cache results for 30 seconds. Cross-tab cache invalidation via BroadcastChannel ensures that when one browser tab updates stock (e.g., processes a receipt), other open tabs reflect the change within the cache TTL. The FHIR base URL is http://medimind.ge/fhir and all monetary values use GEL (Georgian Lari) currency.
